Welcome

In Bouzama, near Essaouira, JNS Villa is a charming villa with a private pool, shaded pergola and large terrace, facing the mountain. Four air-conditioned bedrooms, a garden and housekeeping included: a peaceful, welcoming setting designed for the comfort, privacy and relaxation of your holiday.

Our services

JNS Villa is at your disposal with maximum service to give you an unforgettable holiday. Housekeeping is included during your stay: meal preparation, dishes, general tidying of the rooms, bed linen changes and laundry care.

Your host Ayoub, welcoming, helpful and available, makes sure your stay goes as smoothly as possible and gladly shares his favourite local tips.

The service covers the upkeep and tidying of the villa: guests are asked to put away their own personal belongings and clothes.
